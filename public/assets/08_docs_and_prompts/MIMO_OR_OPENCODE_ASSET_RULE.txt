NON-NEGOTIABLE IMPLEMENTATION RULES

1. Only use services verified on the live JMOTO site.
2. Never use the old V4 import/export, web-design, gates, access-control, smart-home or IT/network imagery.
3. Use authentic JMOTO gallery photos for Projects/Training whenever possible.
4. Generated assets may support page heroes/service cards, but must not claim a job is an actual JMOTO completed project unless it came from the authentic gallery.
5. Desktop and mobile heroes are separate physical files. Do not serve desktop to mobile.
6. Keep image text-free. All titles, pricing, CTAs, phone numbers and translations remain HTML/CSS.
7. Keep the existing official JMOTO logo from the codebase.
8. Product imagery on Shop is illustrative unless a product is actually stocked. Do not attach generated imagery to a specific SKU/brand unless verified.
9. Use loading=lazy below the fold, width/height attributes, and responsive srcset/picture.
10. Before calling the work finished, manually verify every relevant page on desktop and mobile, including Projects gallery, Supply and Shop.
